Customer Churn Analysis
Tools Used

Python (Pandas) — Data Exploration & Insights
Power BI — Dashboard & Visualization

Dataset

Telco Customer Churn Dataset
7,043 customers | 21 columns
Source: Kaggle

What I Did
1. Data Exploration & Insights (Python & Pandas)

Loaded and explored the dataset
Converted TotalCharges from string to float
Created tenure groups (0–1 yr, 1–2 yr, 2–4 yr, 4+ yr)
Used Pandas to calculate churn rates by contract type, internet service, tenure, and monthly charges
Identified high-risk customer segments

2. Power BI Dashboard

KPI Cards — Churn Rate (26.5%), Total Customers (7,043), Churned Customers (1,869)
Bar Chart — Churn by Contract Type
Bar Chart — Churn by Internet Service
Line Chart — Churn vs Monthly Charges
Bar Chart — Churn by Customer Tenure
Donut Chart — Overall Churn Distribution

Key Findings

Overall churn rate is 26.54%
Month-to-month contract customers churn the most
Two-year contract customers almost never churn
Fiber optic users have higher churn than DSL users
Customers with tenure less than 1 year are at highest risk
Churned customers pay higher monthly charges on average ($74.44 vs $61.27)

Recommendation
Offer discounts on long-term contracts to reduce month-to-month churn
Create loyalty programs for new customers in their first year
Investigate fiber optic pricing and service quality
